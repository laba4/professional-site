# Books

Some books I've read and can recommend

## Fiction

- **The Phoenix Project** by Gene Kim et al., about how DevOps turns around a struggling IT department
- **Little House in the Big Woods** by Laura Ingalls Wilder, about Laura's childhood in a log cabin in the Wisconsin woods

## Health

- **Why We Sleep** by Matthew Walker, about why sleep matters and how to sleep better
- **Ernährungskompass** by Bas Kast, about building a healthy diet based on scientific research

## Psychology

- **Man's Search for Meaning** by Viktor Frankl, about finding meaning even in the darkest of times

## Technical

- **Dive Into Design Patterns** by Alexander Shvets, about software design patterns and when to use them
